# Fantasy Worlds

Pick fantasy subgenre and keep material language consistent.

## Subgenres
Medieval low-magic · High fantasy · Dark fantasy · Urban fantasy

## Tenets
1. Material honesty (wood/stone/leather/magic emissive rules)
2. Silhouette heraldry readable
3. Magic FX gated by `vfx-fx`
4. Architecture matches tech level


### Appendix: dark-fantasy

# Dark Fantasy
Broken grandeur, ash, thorns, rusted armor, bleak skies, corrupted nature.

### Appendix: high-fantasy

# High Fantasy
Elegant stone, glowing crystals, clean heraldry, heroic scale, magical infrastructure.

### Appendix: medieval

# Medieval
Timber, thatch, cobble, iron fittings, low cleanliness, hand-made irregularity.

### Appendix: urban-fantasy

# Urban Fantasy
Modern city + hidden magic props, sigils in alleys, neon vs relic contrast.

---


# Sci-Fi / Punk Worlds

Choose one punk/sci-fi lane. Don’t mix chrome cyber with brass steam unless intentional mashup.

## Lanes
Hard sci-fi · Space opera · Cyberpunk · Steampunk · Dieselpunk · Atompunk · Solarpunk · Biopunk · Clockpunk


### Appendix: cyberpunk

# Cyberpunk
Dense verticality, neon signage, rain, cables, megacorp branding, street vs tower contrast.

### Appendix: diesel-atom

# Dieselpunk / Atompunk
Diesel: WWII-ish machines, heavy panels. Atompunk: 1950s future, chrome, atomic motifs, pastel+chrome.

### Appendix: hard-space

# Hard Sci-Fi / Space
Functional panels, readable modules, zero-g cues, subdued materials, realistic wear.

### Appendix: solar-bio-clock

# Solarpunk / Biopunk / Clockpunk
Solarpunk: green cities, warm sun, integrated plants. Biopunk: flesh-tech, wet labs. Clockpunk: gears, clockwork, pre-steam precision.

### Appendix: steampunk

# Steampunk
Brass, copper, rivets, gauges, steam vents, Victorian silhouettes + machines.

---


# Historical Worlds

Period-accurate silhouettes and materials. Document era before kitbashing.

## Groups
Western · Pirate · Samurai/Ninja · Viking · Ancient (Egypt/Greece/Rome) · Victorian · Modern · Military/WW/Cold War


### Appendix: military-eras

# Military Eras
Match kit to conflict: webbing, helmets, vehicles, stencil markings. Avoid anachronistic optics/rails unless alt-history.

### Appendix: period-materials

# Period Materials Cheat
Egypt: stone, gold leaf, linen. Rome: marble, mosaic, bronze. Victorian: brick, iron, wallpaper. Western: wood, dust, leather. WW: olive drab, steel, canvas.

---


# Apocalypse / Dystopia Worlds

Collapse language: scavenged materials, faded brands, improvised repairs.

## Lanes
Post-apoc wasteland · Zombie cities · Alien invasion damage · Dystopian control · Utopian veneer · Post-human ruins


### Appendix: dystopia-utopia

# Dystopia vs Utopia
Dystopia: surveillance, barriers, propaganda. Utopia: clean design that feels too perfect (uncanny).

### Appendix: wasteland

# Wasteland
Bleached palette, rust, tarp shelters, patched vehicles, sparse vegetation.

---


# Biome Worlds

Climate drives palette, fog, vegetation density, and material wear.


### Appendix: biome-cheatsheet

# Biome Cheatsheet
Underwater: caustics, particulate, desaturated distance. Desert: heat haze, dust, sharp shadows. Arctic: long blues, snow cover, breath fog. Jungle: dense canopy, god rays, wet leaves. Underground: practical lamps, wet rock. Floating islands: cliff undersides, mist, vertical composition.
