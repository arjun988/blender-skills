# Noir Style

Shadows as design. Silhouettes. Wet ground mirrors. Limited color or mono.

## Tenets
1. Motivated hard keys / blinds / neon slash
2. Negative fill
3. Smoke/fog shafts
4. Costume readable in silhouette
5. Grade crushed but detailed shadows


### Appendix: noir-lighting

# Noir Lighting
Venetian blind gobos, single practical, rim separation, wet asphalt reflection plane.

---


# Minimalist Style

Reduce until it breaks — then add one thing back.

## Tenets
1. Few shapes
2. Few colors
3. Negative space intentional
4. Typography-like clarity of form
5. Abstract OK if brief asks


### Appendix: reduction-rules

# Reduction Rules
Remove bevels/greebles first. Keep silhouette. Cap materials at 1–3.

---


# Vector Style

Flat fills. Clean edges. Iconic shapes. Almost no gradients unless intentional.


### Appendix: vector-rules

# Vector Rules
Hard color regions. No noisy textures. Bevels minimal. Shadows as graphic shapes.

---


# Chibi Style

Big head. Short body. Maximum appeal silhouette.

## Ratios
Head often 1/2 to 1/3 of total height. Simplify fingers. Large eyes optional by genre.


### Appendix: chibi-proportions

# Chibi Proportions
2–4 heads tall. Neck minimal/hidden. Feet small or rounded stubs. Hands chunky.

---


# Frutiger Aero

Glossy bubbles. Fresh greens/blues. Nature + tech optimism. Soft skeuomorphic forms.


### Appendix: aero-cues

# Aero Cues
Water drops, glass orbs, lush grass, blue skies, glossy white devices, aurora/ribbons, clean sans signage.

---


# Neon / Retrofuturism

Sunset gradients. Grid perspective. Chrome + neon. 80s future memory.


### Appendix: synthwave

# Synthwave
Purple/orange dusk, grid ground, wireframe mountains, hot neon edges, black chrome.

### Appendix: vaporwave

# Vaporwave
Pastel pink/cyan, classical busts, VHS, palm, Japanese type, soft glitch.

---


# Cozy / Wholesome Mood

Warm light. Soft shapes. Safe clutter. Low threat.

## Tenets
1. Warm keys / soft fill
2. Rounded prop language
3. Clutter = comfort, not hazard
4. Gentle palettes
5. Cameras at friendly eye height


### Appendix: cozy-palette

# Cozy Palette
Warm woods, soft pastels or earth tones, practical lamps, steam/food cues, plants alive.

---


# Dark / Gritty Mood

Wear as story. Low key lighting. Melancholy palettes. Industrial weight.


### Appendix: gritty-materials

# Gritty Materials
Rust layers, peeling paint, oil stains, mud height, broken practicals, sparse warm lights in cold scenes.

---


# Dream / Surreal Mood

Logic bent. Scale play. Soft physics. Symbolic props.


### Appendix: surreal-devices

# Surreal Devices
Impossible scale, floating objects, mirrored rooms, physics-defying cloth, color that ignores light logic.

---


# Brutalist Mood

Monumental concrete. Austere. Human small. Few materials.


### Appendix: brutalist-forms

# Brutalist Forms
Board-formed concrete, deep recesses, oversized stairs, slit windows, overcast skylight.
